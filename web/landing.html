Honest limits

What to know before you trust it.

Hackathon prototype on testnet. We would rather say this plainly.

Only routed revenue counts+

Money paid straight to a wallet is invisible to RevVault and is not collateral. Customers must pay through the pay link.

Repayment is economic, not legal+

A business can stop routing payments. Limits stay small and a fee-funded reserve takes the first loss.

APY is illustrative+

Figures come from fees only and are not a promise of yield.

The demo runs on testnet with mock USDC+

The app talks to the deployed testnet contracts through a wallet. Use a throwaway testnet account, never a personal one. Mock USDC, no real funds, and the contracts are unaudited.

Notes are pool-held economic claims+

A revenue note records one loan and follows its repayment. In this MVP the pool holds every note. It is not a security and it cannot be transferred yet.

A history score only sets the rate+

A wallet-history score can move the advance rate within a narrow band. It never adds revenue and never creates a starter limit.

Creator income counts only if routed+

Tips, sponsor payments and commissions count only when they are paid through the RevVault pay link. Income paid anywhere else is invisible to the vault.
